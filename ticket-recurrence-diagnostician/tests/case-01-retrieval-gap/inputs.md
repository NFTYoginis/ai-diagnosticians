# Case 01 — inputs

Constructed case. Paste everything below into a project running the diagnostician, then say
"Diagnose this ticket pattern."

## Subject topic

**Transferring a project to another workspace.** Six-week window. 34 tickets. 610 active users
of the feature over the window (598 at the start, 610 at the end).

### The article

Title: **"Moving projects between workspaces"**

Published 14 months ago. Last edited 11 months ago. Linked from the help center's Projects
category and from the workspace settings page.

Opening paragraph: "You can move a project from one workspace to another if you are an owner of
both. Moving a project brings its boards, files, and comment history with it. Members who are
not in the destination workspace will lose access."

Steps 1 through 5 use *move* and *workspace* throughout. Step 3 reads: "Open the project's
overflow menu and choose **Move project**."

### The product

The control in the product interface is labelled **Transfer**. It was renamed from **Move
project** in a release thirteen months ago. The article was not updated.
