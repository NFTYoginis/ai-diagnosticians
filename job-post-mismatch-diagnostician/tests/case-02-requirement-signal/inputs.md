"Diagnose this requisition."

## The requisition

- **Senior Data Engineer**
- Hybrid, three days onsite, one metro
- Posted range **$155,000 – $175,000**, raised from **$145,000 – $165,000 on day 19**
- **44 days open**
- Running on three channels: a general board, a niche data board, and the company careers page

### The day-19 change

The band was raised and **the post was republished as a new listing** rather than edited in
place, so both boards re-ranked and re-notified. Nothing else changed: no title edit, no
requirement change, no new channel, no sponsorship change, no work-model change.

Both general channels index a posted range on its lower bound and offer minimum-salary filters in
$25,000 increments above $100,000. The new lower bound of $155,000 crosses the $150,000 increment
the old one sat under.

### The post

Title: **"Senior Data Engineer"**

Requirement block — **19 items in a single list, no separation of required from preferred**, in
this order:

1. Airflow · 2. dbt · 3. Snowflake · 4. Spark · 5. Kafka · 6. Terraform · 7. Build and maintain
reporting datasets · 8. Partner with analysts on dashboard requirements · 9. Own data quality
checks for BI models · 10. SQL at an advanced level · 11. Python · 12. **Production ownership of a
streaming pipeline** · 13. Git and code review practice · 14. Comfortable in a hybrid team ·
15. Documentation habit · 16. dbt testing · 17. **Five years in data engineering** · 18. Familiarity
with data contracts · 19. Curiosity

Items 12 and 17 are the two must-haves the screen actually applies.

### Where the failures land

Of the 217 submissions that did not pass, **214 missed on item 12, production ownership of a
streaming pipeline.** The remaining 3 missed on item 17. No other stated requirement accounts for
any failure.

Eleven candidates reached first contact through employee referral. They are tracked separately and
are not in any figure above.

## Written screening rules, as applied

> Screen in if: (a) five or more years in a data engineering title, AND (b) has owned a streaming
> pipeline in production, evidenced anywhere in the submission. Everything else in the post is
> preferred. No other criterion is applied at this stage.
