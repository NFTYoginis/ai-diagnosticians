### The day-34 change

The band was raised and **the range was edited in place on the existing postings.** Neither
channel re-notified, re-ranked, or re-surfaced the listings, and the impression series shows no
change of shape at the edit date.

Both channels index a posted range on its lower bound and offer minimum-salary filters in $10,000
increments. $112,000 and $118,000 both sit between the $110,000 and $120,000 increments.
