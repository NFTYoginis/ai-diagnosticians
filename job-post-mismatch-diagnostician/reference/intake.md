# Intake

What a case needs before a diagnosis is possible, and what to do when it is missing.

**Read the boundary in `rules.md` before assembling a case.** Nothing on this page asks for an
application, a résumé, a portfolio, or a candidate name, and nothing on this page can be
substituted with one.

---

## Required

Without all four, the funnel cannot be reconstructed and the correct output is **Undetermined**.

1. **The post as published**, on every channel it ran on, with its edit history and dates. If it
   reads differently on different channels, that difference is evidence.
2. **Channel-level funnel data** — impressions, views, apply-starts, and submissions, by channel
   and by week, plus sponsorship spend wherever any was used.
3. **A comparison set of four or more requisitions** — same company where possible, adjacent
   level, comparable band, location, work model, window, and channel mix, with requirement
   structure allowed to vary. See `baselines.md`.
4. **Aggregate screening counts** — for each stated must-have, how many submissions met it,
   **broken out by channel**. These come from your own screening. Do not supply applications.

The fourth is the one teams most often try to substitute for, and the substitution is always the
same: "here are the applications, you tell us." That is the one thing this folder will not do.
The by-channel breakdown is also the item most often supplied pooled, and pooled is not enough —
it is the only thing that separates the two live causes at Stage 4.

---

## Strongly wanted

Each of these decides a specific branch. Note in the report which were absent.

- **The written screening rules or rubric as applied**, including any criterion not stated in the
  post. This is what decides 4D, and it is a document, not a judgment.
- **Withdrawal and no-show counts after first contact**, with reasons grouped in aggregate.
- **Time from submission to first contact**, and the same for the comparison set.
- **Competing posts visible in the same search results** — title, stated range, work model,
  requirement structure. This is the active comparison, and it is what Stage 2 runs on.
- **Days open**, for the subject and every comparison requisition.
- **The compensation range as posted, and whether any edit was republished or re-surfaced.**
  Without the second half, a compensation change cannot be evaluated at all.
- **The channel's salary indexing and filter behaviour**, from the channel's own documentation.
  Threshold exclusion cannot be confirmed without it.
- **The application form itself** — field count, required materials, and where drop-off
  concentrates.

---

## Useful

- Board placement or search-position data
- The requisition's approved band versus the posted range
- Prior cycles of the same requisition
- Referral and internal-transfer volume, so it can be routed outside the funnel
- The hiring manager's written must-haves, where they differ from the post
- Sector news for the window: layoffs, competitor hiring waves, seasonal cycles

## Handling missing evidence

**Never substitute inference for a missing input.** The failure mode this folder exists to
prevent is a confident cause built on absent data — and the second one is a confident cause built
on a pile of applications nobody should be reading.

| Missing | Effect | What to write |
|---|---|---|
| All channel-level funnel data | Break cannot be located | Undetermined. Name it as the single blocking input. |
| Impressions | Stage 1 cannot be tested comparatively | Report which stages you can test. Eliminate Stage 1 structurally if Stage 2 is at baseline, and label the elimination structural. |
| Apply-starts | Stage 3 cannot be tested comparatively | Same treatment. Structural elimination where Stage 4 volume is healthy, named as structural rather than comparative. |
| Per-channel screening counts, pooled only | The Stage 4 discriminator is unavailable | Report requirement signaling and channel-population mismatch as **tied**. Name the by-channel breakdown as the single input that would separate them. Do not pick one. |
| The channel's filter and indexing behaviour | Threshold exclusion cannot be confirmed | Say so. Threshold exclusion stays tied with the other live Stage 1 causes. Do not assert an increment you were not given. |
| Whether a compensation edit was re-surfaced | The change cannot be evaluated | Treat the change as Uninformative and name this as the reason. |
| Written screening rules | 4D cannot be tested | Say that a screen-versus-post disagreement could not be checked, and that it remains live. |
| Post-contact withdrawal data | Stage 5 cannot be tested | If the break is at Stage 5, report Undetermined for the branch split. |
| Comparison set under 4 | Baseline unreliable | Provisional at best. Say the set is too small to establish a range. |
| Comparison set at a different band, location, or work model | The set does not address the same population | Usable with limitations at best, and frequently Not usable. Say which. |
| Market or prior-cycle data for the null | Null untestable | State that the null could not be rejected, which caps confidence at Provisional regardless of how clean the rest is. |

The last row matters most. If you cannot test the null, you cannot claim a positive diagnosis at
full confidence, because the most likely alternative explanation was never examined.

## The post itself

The post is primary evidence at several branches. Read it **as published, on each channel**, not
as drafted and not as the team remembers it. A description of a post is not evidence about the
post.

What to look at, and only in the branches where it is live:

- **Title and stated range only, for Stage 1.** Would this be returned by the searches the target
  population runs, and does its lower bound clear the increments those searches use? Nothing about
  the body is in evidence at this stage.
- **The listing card and the first screen, for Stage 2.** What is visible above the fold on a
  phone. Whether the work model and the range are stated. Whether the opening describes the job or
  the company. Compare against the competing posts in the same results.
- **The requirement block, for Stage 4.** How many items. Whether required and preferred are
  separated. Whether the list is internally consistent with the title and the level. Whether any
  item is a proxy for something else. Which attributes it foregrounds, and which job family shares
  those attributes.

A long requirement list is not on its own a diagnosis. Plenty of requisitions with long lists fill
quickly. The question is always whether the list explains the specific stage that broke.

## Privacy and scope

**No applicant names, contact details, résumés, CVs, cover letters, portfolios, work samples,
assessment results, or interview notes.** Not needed, not read, not reproduced. If candidate
material appears in what is supplied, the report states that it was supplied and not read, and
does not quote or summarize it. Redacting a name does not make assessment acceptable; the
boundary is about evaluating a person, not about knowing who they are.

Screening figures are aggregate counts. Recruiters, screeners, and hiring managers are referred
to by role or not at all, and no finding is written about a named person.

No demographic analysis, and no inference about any protected characteristic, from any input.
Adverse impact analysis is a compliance function with its own methodology and its own legal
exposure, and it is out of scope here.
